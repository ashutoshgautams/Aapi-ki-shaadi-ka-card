# Images

Drop files here with these exact names and they appear automatically. Every
slot is optional — a missing file falls back to a plain tinted surface, never
to drawn ornament, so the page never looks broken while you are still
generating.

## Keep the whole set in one visual world

Append this to **every** prompt so the set matches `doors.jpg`:

> White Makrana marble with pale ice-blue inlaid panels and aged brass detail,

## `hero.jpg` — what lies beyond the doors  ← generate this first

Portrait, at least 1400 × 2000. It sits behind the couple's names, so the
**middle of the frame must be calm** — sky, haze, or plain marble. Detail
belongs at the edges.

> A Mughal palace courtyard seen from inside a shaded arcade, looking out
> through a large scalloped archway onto an open marble terrace and soft pale
> blue sky beyond. Symmetrical, centred, deep perspective, empty of people.
> Morning haze and long soft shadows across the marble floor.

## `corridor.jpg` — the colonnade they walk

Landscape or square, at least 2000 wide. The couple move across the lower
third, so keep the **bottom half uncluttered**.

> A long marble colonnade in a Mughal palace, receding into soft light, rows of
> scalloped arches on both sides, polished marble floor with gentle
> reflections. Centred one-point perspective, empty, serene, bright and airy.

## `bride.png` / `groom.png` — the two figures

**Transparent background, PNG.** Full body, at least 1200 tall, feet at the
very bottom edge of the canvas.

> bride: A South Asian bride photographed **from behind**, full length,
> standing, wearing an ice-blue and gold gharara with a long embroidered
> dupatta trailing from her head. Face not visible at all. Isolated on a fully
> transparent background, soft daylight from the left.

> groom: A South Asian groom photographed **from behind**, full length,
> standing, wearing an ivory sherwani with gold buttonwork, a pale blue stole
> over one shoulder and a turban. Face not visible at all. Isolated on a fully
> transparent background, soft daylight from the left.

If you cannot get clean transparency, send the raw image and it can be cut out
here. Without these two files Act II still runs on the corridor and the
narration — nothing stands in for them.
